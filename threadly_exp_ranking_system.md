# Space Experience & Ranking System

## 1. Overview

Space rewards users for community participation through an **Experience (EXP) system**.  
EXP is earned for positive contributions and can be lost through **moderation, rule violations, or excessive downvotes**.  

- **EXP** → tracks activity and contribution; can increase or decrease.  
- **Level** → milestone achievement based on EXP; never decreases.  
- **Titles** → visual badges representing achievement.  
- **Unique Titles** → special recognition for elite or rare accomplishments.  

---

## 2. Earning EXP

| Action | EXP Earned | Notes |
|--------|------------|-------|
| Create a Thread (Post) | 10 | Base EXP |
| Thread Upvote | 2 per upvote | Encourages quality content |
| Thread marked Helpful / Pinned | 15–30 | Moderator bonus |
| Reply to Thread | 5 | Base EXP |
| Reply Upvote | 1–2 | Per upvote |
| Sub-reply (Nested comment) | 3 | Slightly less than main reply |
| Daily Login | 2–5 | Streak bonus possible |
| First Post of the Day | 2 | Encourages daily participation |
| Create a Space | 20 | Community-building bonus |
| Accepted Answer / Solution | 20 | Reward for helpful contribution |
| Moderation-approved contribution | 5–10 | Optional for flagged helpful posts |

**Notes:**  
- Daily EXP cap: **100–150 EXP/day**  
- Diminishing returns after multiple low-effort posts/replies  
- Self-upvotes do **not** give EXP  

---

## 3. Losing EXP

| Action | EXP Lost | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Thread or Reply deleted by moderator | 10–25 | Depending on severity |
| Spam / duplicate content | 10–30 | Escalating penalties for repeat offenders |
| Rule violations (toxic, offensive) | 15+ | Can accumulate |

**Downvotes & Locked EXP Rules:**  
- **Downvotes can punish posts**, but gently:  
  - Downvotes **do not remove EXP already earned**  
  - Downvotes can **cancel or reduce future locked EXP**  
  - Posts reaching a **net negative threshold** (e.g., -5) may cancel all pending EXP for that post  
  - Severe repeated downvotes may trigger **soft EXP clawback** (-5 to -15 EXP per post)  
- Locked EXP is only awarded if the post survives **24–48 hours** without hitting negative thresholds  
- Inactivity for short periods does **not** remove EXP  
- EXP loss is tied to **behavior**, not community opinion

## 5. Optional Advanced Mechanics

**Locked EXP**  
- Initial EXP for a Thread or Reply is partially **locked** until the post is approved by moderation or survives **24–48 hours**.  
- Downvotes during this period can **cancel the locked EXP** if thresholds are reached.  
- Prevents spam while rewarding quality contribution.  

**Downvote Penalty System**  
- Net score ≤ -3 → EXP from post frozen  
- Net score ≤ -5 → Locked EXP canceled, base EXP may be clawed back  
- Net score ≤ -8 → Optional soft EXP clawback (-5 to -15 EXP)  
- Repeated heavily downvoted posts may trigger moderation review
